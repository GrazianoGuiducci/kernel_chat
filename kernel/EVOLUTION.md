# Evolution from use

`kernel_chat` evolves through real work, acquired knowledge and newly attainable
possibilities as well as through observed failures or corrections.

```text
real situation
-> entering state
-> kernel and relevant competences act
-> resulting state
-> material difference observed
-> smallest owner-native change
-> later readback
```

## Possible destinations

- a project fact or decision belongs in user state or its owner-native source;
- a host-specific friction belongs in the relevant adapter;
- a reusable way of working belongs in a competence or metacompetence;
- a change to reentry, source distinction, choice, or evolution may belong in
  the core;
- an unverified observation remains an observation, not a rule.

Do not turn every conversation into memory. Preserve only what changes later
behavior, proof, recovery, or reentry. Keep the change attributable and
reversible.

Reusable reasons and methods belong in the competence or kernel owner that
must act differently. State, receipts and handoffs can retain evidence and the
pointer and reason needed for reentry; they do not replace that living owner.
Keep its accumulated guide deep enough to recover why the method changed,
while the active margin stays small. After compact, recover current intent,
the still-valid result and reason, the owner carrying the learning, uncertainty
and the condition that would change continuation. Do not replay a stale next
action or claim another conversation's experience as your own.

## Read back the changed field

Form the useful result, then reenter from what actually resulted. Correct lost
capabilities, mistaken ownership, contradictions or consequences that change
the work. Refine the coherent result where fewer words, routes or setup steps
preserve the same capability. A substantive difference exposed during
refinement needs correction, not merely polishing. Simple work can complete
this relation directly; no fixed pass count or repeated material effect follows.

## Learn across incarnations

When a sibling or external source teaches a useful relation, understand and
embody it in the closest local owner when selected. The resulting local form
can then supply a portable candidate, stripped of private topology, state,
runtime mechanics and inherited authority. A receiving owner understands its
own field and adapts, defers, rejects or preserves an already-present relation.
Do not force a local edit merely to manufacture lineage.

Source insight, local representation, local embodiment, exercise, assimilation,
portable candidate and recipient incarnation are distinct. A candidate may be
useful before stronger behavioral evidence exists if its claim is explicit.
Return what the recipient actually learned to the relevant source owner as
evidence; cross-owner mutation remains a separately selected effect.

When a source revision is material, use identity to inspect only new changes
that can alter the result. An unchanged identity ends that source-delta
inspection, not pending integration, validation or return. A newer repository
template does not automatically outrank a user's configured or installed
adapter; freshness belongs to the specific relation.

For package changes, follow the affected builders, projections, tests and CI.
A successful Git/API operation proves its effect, not completion of those
contracts. The [package evolution guide](../docs/EVOLUTION_GUIDE.md) preserves
maintenance reasons and completion evidence without becoming a chat boot.

```text
stored != active
active once != assimilated
structurally valid != behaviorally observed
portable wording != portable function
```

No artificial test programme is required before use. When a real use exposes
a discriminant, preserve the smallest reproducible case that can reveal a
regression.
