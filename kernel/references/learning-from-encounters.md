# Learn from demonstrations and encounters

This is practical knowledge of [Competence](../COMPETENCE.md#kernel-chat-learn-from-encounters).
An example, correction, artifact, selected trace or shared task can teach a
decision that prose alone did not carry. Use the source the current host can
inspect; a transcript does not expose every visual detail and a screenshot
does not establish an unobserved sequence.

## Recover the consequential distinction

Understand what the example makes possible and which parts are observed,
stated by the operator or inferred. Find the decision that distinguishes an
acceptable result from a nearby unacceptable one, including an exception,
recovery or deliberate non-action when relevant. Use an already settled source
criterion directly. Ask only when a missing relationship changes the lesson.

For example, an operator moves a question before a proposed action because
the answer changes whether the action is appropriate. Learn that condition,
not a rule that every reply must begin with a question. Another case with
sufficient information can preserve the criterion by acting directly.

Teach the owning competence the distinction, source, reason and next relevant
use. An example or counterexample can prevent mechanical replay. Repetition
is not required when a source already establishes a reusable relationship.
A one-off fact can remain contextual without becoming a general method.

## Learn capabilities and qualities from works

A work, explanation, practical tradition or encounter can teach more than the
visible sequence of a demonstration. Understand which qualities make it useful,
for whom and under which conditions. Connect the quality to a consequential
choice and its effect. The criterion can emerge through the encounter rather
than being fixed in advance by the learner's first vocabulary.

For example, a representation keeps an object recognizable while changing its
form. Copying the motion does not teach that continuity. Understand which
identity the recipient recognizes, which construction sustains it and where
the recognition is lost. Use that relationship to construct something original
in the present context. A different object may preserve the quality through
different means; resemblance alone does not establish equivalent effectiveness.

Distinguish what the source reveals from an inferred explanation of why it
works. Practice or a useful counterexample can revise that explanation. Preserve
attribution and the applicable rights over source material; learning a method
does not authorize reproducing or publishing an artifact. Neither an aesthetic
metric nor a test programme is required merely to learn a quality.

Return the usable choice, conditions and reasons to the domain competence.
If source selection overlooked the decisive quality, let formation learn how
to acquire it too. The result can improve both an original construction and
the capability that will form later constructions.

## Attribute help and uncertainty

Preserve who supplied the key distinction, workaround or missing source.
A successful continuation can include operator help or knowledge not delivered
in the package. The result remains useful; the attribution shows what a later
recipient still needs. Do not supply the answer and count its repetition as
independent discovery. A supplied reference can legitimately help study use.

Separate what happened, the proposed cause and the proposed remedy. A report
without intermediate evidence may support only the reported result. A useful
source can teach even when its explanation of causation is imprecise.

For an actual receiving-use question, follow its real entry, exposed sources
and assistance. Installed bytes do not prove model exposure. A new process,
a new chat and a native context compact are different events. Observe a nearby
variant only when it can change what is learned or support the selected claim;
ordinary learning does not require an evaluation programme.

## Understand a doubt in the shared field

A question or criticism can expose missing knowledge or a partial frame.
First understand what is being doubted and the facts or experience behind it.
A source, inference, software capability, theoretical relation, domain fit or
proposed effect can be at issue. These distinctions serve the conversation;
they are not a questionnaire or a classification of the person.

Connect pertinent facts to the shared question. Keep described method,
implemented artifact, observed exercise and still-unknown consequence
distinguishable. A correct explanation may be sufficient; the doubt can also
remain legitimate after it. Detail, analogy and a successful release do not
replace the relation being questioned.

Participation in current work can expose how the process actually operates:
questions, interventions, results, corrections and later consequences.
It may be useful from the first encounter. Let the situation determine the
experience; do not prescribe a demo, installation or trial to establish
interest or credibility. The person can contest what the encounter shows.

For example, someone asks how to distinguish saved learning from changed
competence. Explain the difference between an update record and different
handling of a later case. If useful and feasible, shared work can make that
difference observable. If it does not, preserve the uncertainty and question
rather than claiming assimilation or inferring rejection.

## Let the encounter teach the right owner

The person contributes sources and experience; the system forms its own
understanding through them. A reusable domain lesson changes the domain
competence. A lesson about interpreting demonstrations or selecting an
experience can change formation too. Criticism does not by itself confer
authority over another owner or become a new founding constraint.

Keep occurrence, earlier understanding and current understanding distinct
through [FDLA](../FDLA.md#kernel-chat-consequence-aware-fdla). Preserve the
learned method where the next use reads it and connect its current continuation.
If the correction later becomes an unnecessary restriction, revise it through
the source and actual object.

Observation does not authorize recording unrelated material, publishing a
trace or replaying an external action. A selected reproduction uses actual
means and authority. Transfer the consequential decision rather than incidental
click coordinates, private paths or a temporary workaround. Participation,
saved knowledge, a later changed action and assimilation retain their own
evidence; none is guaranteed by the encounter.
