# Practical competence formation and inheritance

This is working knowledge of [Competence](../COMPETENCE.md#kernel-chat-practical-formation).
Use it when a capacity needs usable knowledge, deeper method or a continuing
form. The present work can be an inquiry, a domain or a new kernel; a project
and a prior failure are not prerequisites.

## Understand what should become possible

Recover the actual situation, operator relation, useful existing capacities
and what the work could make possible. A requested artifact can express only
part of that intent. Follow its function and reasons when they change the
question; preserve a sufficient determination when they do not.

Locate the knowledge that changes the ability to act: a distinction, causal
explanation, decision criterion, example, exception, practical means or recovery
relation. Qualified sources can teach both the answer and how to find a better
answer. A source title or a promise that the assistant can learn later does not
supply know-how already needed by this capacity.

For example, a source reader returns correct headings while a domain competence
needs the choices and reasons behind them. Read the passages that teach those
decisions. Improve the reader's selection method and, if formation selected the
wrong depth, improve that criterion too. More extracted text is useful only if
it carries the missing relationship.

## Let the reachable field change pertinence

When the intended capacity is still forming, or the same frame selected all
sources and what would count as completion, recover a complementary view of
what can contribute. Consider functions, relations and receiving purposes
beyond the initial topic vocabulary. Use existing orientation when it is
sufficient; this is not a full catalogue scan before every task.

A source or another causal plane can make a previously unrecognized competence
pertinent. Reach its actual body and useful guide, understand the contribution
and let it change the question, method or criterion when warranted. It need
not first prove its value inside the frame it may change. An index helps locate
knowledge; the current list and what the receiver has perceived are smaller
than the field of possible contribution.

For example, a request to improve repeated answers may first suggest a
formatting method. A domain source can reveal that interpreting the input is
the consequential decision. Preserve any useful formatting work while letting
that knowledge change the question and which competence should carry it.
A coherent answer under the first criterion does not settle whether that
criterion fits the intended ability.

Keep the real object and valid determinations while changing observation
through a useful source, owner, scale, representation or receiving condition.
An unexamined relation remains unexamined, not an automatic no_change. Another
view that adds no material difference can support continuing directly.
Openness does not require inventing alternatives or undoing a sound result.

Carry the contribution into the work: why it becomes pertinent now, which
knowledge changes a decision, the actual means and where its consequence
teaches the continuing owner. An existing guide can retain that focus.
Adding names to a trace or recording more context does not perform this
connection. A known method displaced by the assistant's frame needs corrected
use; it does not automatically require another body in the receiving kernel.

## Let acquisition change the forming method

When consolidating substantial knowledge, acquire its meaning, recompose the
relations in the field changed by that acquisition, and consolidate what now
changes the continuing method. These passages can reveal another question,
source, collaborator or organization. They are not three reviews of every act.

Consolidation makes useful depth reachable, resolves competing active
instructions and removes duplication while preserving reasons. Correct the
first body or entry that loses the relation; a new high-level explanation
cannot cancel the opposite instruction encountered later.

Let the forming competence understand its contribution to the larger work:
what it can notice, understand and do, why its decisions matter, which means
are actually usable, and where its result continues. A competence need not be
an executor of decisions made elsewhere. Its understanding can change the
question and the composition while the work is forming.

## Let a principle change an ability

Keep original words, observed occurrence and the assistant's interpretation
distinguishable. A generalizable relation can become a principle when its
scope and reasons make later choices more capable. An unresolved formulation
can remain unresolved. A useful principle does not become a founding axiom
or a universal command by being saved.

A competence owns continuing knowledge and method. An ability is what one or
more competences can make exercisable in an actual context through real means.
Teach the concrete consumer how the principle changes its choice. A detached
list of principles cannot perform that change.

Suppose a correction teaches that a source's date determines which policy it
describes. Preserve the source qualification in the domain method. If the
generator routinely treats source labels as timeless authority, teach it to
recover those conditions too. The two lessons can have different owners.

Representing an ability, making it reachable, exercising it and observing later
assimilation are different observations. A source-bound principle can improve
the present method without proving a change to hidden model weights.

## Let a possibility mature

A useful relation can be understood before its final form, owner or return
condition is known. Preserve enough source, reason and reachable depth for it
to remain useful. A recognizable condition can help it return to attention;
an uncertain condition can stay uncertain. No timer, registry or new file is
required merely to preserve that possibility.

When it becomes pertinent again, understand it with current intent, sources,
consequences and means. Accurate memories can describe conditions that never
coexisted. Returning to attention does not authorize an earlier proposed action.
Maturation concerns what can now be understood, chosen or formed; elapsed time
and repeated mention do not establish it.

For example, a retained reading method may become useful in another environment.
Understand that environment's actual reader. Adapting an existing means may
suffice; another helper may now help. The saved possibility does not decide
which implementation follows. An independent determined action can continue
while this possibility remains open.

## Give the knowledge a usable continuation

Reuse, deepen, compose or form the owner that can carry the real contribution.
One competence can carry several functions; one function can need several
owners. Neither the creator's directory tree nor the number of existing bodies
determines the receiving form.

Give the next capable assistant enough meaning and practical knowledge to
conduct the work: the consequential distinctions, reasons, sources, means,
important exceptions and what would change the method. Its existing body and
guide may suffice. Connect its native entry to the depth that becomes pertinent.
If the body is sufficient but was not reached, repair that connection. If the
method was understood but displaced by the assistant's frame, correct the use.

Use the acquired understanding in the present work. If an intermediate passage
is still being learned, conduct it through the acting competence and return
the useful method there. Preserve meaningful knowledge while it forms so a
later reentry does not inherit only a status and a list of paths.

## Form a kernel that can continue forming

A recipient can inherit both the knowledge that does work and the knowledge
that forms how work is done. Give it an entry its actual agent can read, the
working knowledge reached there, and an adequate surface for continuing
experience. A small inquiry may need one skill and its guide; another context
may benefit from cooperating bodies and shared sources.

Transfer the practical relation for understanding intent and sources, composing
capacities, correcting a misleading interpretation, acting through actual
means and returning learning. If the decisive bridge exists only in the
creator's explanation, teach it to the receiving competence or preserve the
gap explicitly. Complete delivery of a selected list does not establish that
the selection meets the intended capacity.

The recipient can change its organization and its criteria of sufficiency.
The creator's current task, private knowledge, tools and authority do not
become its circumstances. A compiler is one possible means; a capable receiver
can exercise formation directly through readable sources.

## Reconcile what returns

New knowledge meets a living recipient that has learned independently. Preserve
useful contributions from both. When material, compare the known received
base, current local method and new proposal, including references and consumers.
An unknown base remains unknown; newer wording and textual merge do not decide
semantic fit. [Evolution](../EVOLUTION.md#kernel-chat-regenerate-kernel) owns
the continuing reconciliation.

A domain lesson returns to its domain owner. A lesson about source selection,
translation, organization or discovery also changes formation when that method
must act differently. Apply it in the next pertinent movement; do not leave
the only change in an improvement log. Later non-identical use can confirm or
correct the learning without becoming a gate for learning now.
