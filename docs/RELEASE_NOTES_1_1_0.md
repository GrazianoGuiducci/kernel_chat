# kernel_chat 1.1.0 — source candidate

This candidate deepens how a user-owned kernel forms practical capabilities
and lets them continue independently. It starts from
`82a5bd78f5ecb667e6392e1dd149be2c34c4ec3c`; candidate source does not claim a
published 1.1.0 release.

## What changes in use

- **Form usable methods.** Acquire the distinctions and reasons a capacity
  needs, let them change the question and selection criteria, and teach the
  result to both the domain and the generator where pertinent.
- **Continue principles and possibilities.** Let a principle change a concrete
  ability. Retain useful source and reason while a possibility's final form is
  still open, then encounter it in the changed present.
- **Learn decisions from encounters.** Interpret demonstrations, examples,
  corrections and doubts through the decision they teach, preserving assistance
  and uncertainty. A shared task can teach without becoming required onboarding.
- **Shape the reachable source field.** Understand canonical, shared, private,
  local and projected knowledge through actual owners and access. Compose
  independent kernels when their contributions help, preserving their learning.
- **Continue an activity competently.** Keep originating purpose distinct from
  mutable method and from an actual controller's configuration.
- **Form truthful presence when useful.** Keep identity, reachable knowledge,
  present capability, open possibility, representation and authority clear.

The existing owners remain the operating core. Five on-demand knowledge bodies
provide practical depth; AGENTS reaches them through the owner pertinent to
the movement. Core also adapts competence-trace density to the actual work.

## Compatibility and evidence

Root source version is `1.1.0`, an additive minor evolution of the stable
semantic operating contract. Portable entry remains `3.1.0`, ChatGPT adapter
`1.0.0` and INSTANCE schema `kernel_chat.instance.v1`.

Configuration/receipt mechanics, user state and installed account instructions
are unchanged. A locally evolved kernel reconciles the source with its own
methods and references rather than resetting them. Source adoption and a host
instruction replacement remain separate effects.

## Publication boundary

The [candidate review](CANDIDATE_1_1_0.md) records design and validation scope.
Structural delivery and CommonMark navigation checks support package claims;
they do not establish model exposure, behavior, transfer benefit or assimilation.
Canonical promotion, tag, release and deployment retain their own effects.
