# Evolution

## Recognition

Work may expose a correction, missing relation, competence gap, new function,
host adapter, representation collision or product form. The first result is an
assessment, not automatic mutation.

## Movement

```text
source-linked signal
-> observed and desired behavior
-> existing faculty composition
-> candidate or retained unknown when needed
-> baseline/candidate comparison
-> independent or non-isomorphic evaluation when material
-> verified_improvement | no_change | regression | tradeoff | unverified
-> owner-selected integration
-> receipt, recovery and reentry delta
```

An improvement may change a state file, rule, competence, skill, deterministic
function, adapter, schema or the kernel itself. Do not predetermine the level.

## De-kernelization

A kernel relation can be revised, demoted or removed when its source changes,
its burden exceeds its benefit, it becomes ritual, or later evidence shows a
simpler faithful composition. Preserve the evidence and recovery relation;
do not protect a component merely because it is called a kernel.
