Add sources only when their distinct ownership or evidence role matters. Do
not treat a source's availability as automatic activation or authority.

A user-owned competence can be linked here at its real location, with its owner
and the condition that makes its method useful. Keep the method and accumulated
learning there. Core kernel owners are reached separately through `AGENTS.md`;
do not copy them into this source table.

A revision may help detect changes when material. An unchanged revision ends
only inspection for new source changes, not already-open integration or work.
