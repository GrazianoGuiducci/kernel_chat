# Continue an activity through competent work

This method connects [Competence](../kernel/COMPETENCE.md#kernel-chat-continuing-activities)
with operational continuity. An activity may continue through ordinary reentry,
real events or scheduled wakes. Its purpose and the actual receiving means
determine its form; a scheduler is not required.

## Preserve purpose while the method learns

Keep the originating purpose and reasons distinguishable from working
instructions that can improve. Recover the current determination from its
actual source, including later operator changes and entrusted flexibility.
A better prompt, new source or successful summary does not by itself replace
that purpose. When the operator changes it, preserve the new determination.

Use the existing authorized continuation surface. It may be a competence guide,
project knowledge or one prompt field in a controller. Purpose and mutable
method can remain logically distinct in that field; separate files or a global
activity registry are not required.

## Make the continuing work competent

When the assignment forms, let the domain competence and its knowledge
participate before a generic prompt fixes the question and method. Its small
entry should reach the current purpose, still-causal result, live source and
guide, and the means needed for the next useful movement.

At a later entry use what has been learned. Changed sources, consequences or
possibilities can reform the question and composition. An unchanged source
can still support deeper understanding or useful no_change. A wake does not
choose the old conclusion or require an output, notification or self-change.

For example, a recurring review learns a better source-comparison method.
Put that method in its domain competence. Change the job entry only if that
connection needs to change. Editing only the prompt can alter one execution
without preserving the useful method for other domain work.

## Return each difference to its owner

Domain knowledge and method belong to the domain competence. A better way of
forming or connecting activities can change this method and competence
formation. Native job identity, trigger and executable settings belong to the
real controller. A current point or deferred decision remains in its actual
continuation surface, with the reason needed to recover deeper knowledge.

When sufficient knowledge existed but did not participate, repair the entry
instead of duplicating its body. Saving a report does not establish that
another owner read or used it. A shared result can teach several owners through
different specific returns.

## Distinguish what is being resumed

A retained possibility returns to understanding in the changed present. A
decided effect continues from its still-valid source, target, scope and
authority. An uncertain outcome requires observing the actual controller before
retrying. Use [Receipts and recovery](RECEIPTS_RECOVERY.md) for that relation.

Where material, preserve completed, pending, failed and uncertain operations
separately with pointers to real receipts. A pending operation need not already
have a receipt. Continue unresolved work without reopening unchanged authority.
An actual changed effect requires resolving that changed relationship.

## Change a real controller when its effect is selected

Establish the controller, exact job, receiving context, exposed fields and
applicable authority. A copied prompt or another host's capability does not
establish them. A source-selection change, cadence, enabled state, model,
other jobs and external actions can have different authority and means.

Within the entrusted change, retain the previous configuration needed for
recovery, make the specific alteration through native means and read the
controller back. Preserve the complete relevant installed instructions and
configuration in a suitable private or user-controlled recovery surface.
A summary of an update does not preserve the exact native object.

If the controller cannot expose or apply the requested field, keep that
unapplied difference explicit and continue independent useful work. A configured
job does not prove a trigger, execution, receipt by another owner or later
improvement. A recovery copy does not migrate or run the scheduler.

## Reconsider usefulness through real consequences

Repeated work can reveal lost access, duplicate effort, an unreceived lesson
or another useful capacity. Quiet output and unchanged sources alone do not
show that an activity lacks value. Conversely, genuine completion can remain
complete. A simpler method, different connection or retirement can become
appropriate; changing a native job is its own effect.

This reflection is part of the activity's competent work. It does not create
a score, evaluator or periodic review obligation. Continue from the result
and teach the specific reusable difference to the owner whose later work
should change.
